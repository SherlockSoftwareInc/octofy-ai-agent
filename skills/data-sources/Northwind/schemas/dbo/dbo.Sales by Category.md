# View: [dbo].[Sales by Category]

**Data Source:** Northwind
**Schema:** dbo
**Type:** View

## Description

# **View:** `[dbo].[Sales by Category]`
> Aggregates total sales revenue for each product, grouped by product category. Provides a summary view of sales performance across different food categories.
---
### **Columns:**
| Ord | Name | Data Type | Description |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 1 | `CategoryID` | INTEGER | Unique identifier for the product category. Reference: Categories.CategoryID |
| 2 | `CategoryName` | NVARCHAR(15) | Name of the product category (e.g., Beverages, Condiments, Dairy Products). |
| 3 | `ProductName` | NVARCHAR(40) | Name of the individual product within the category. |
| 4 | `ProductSales` | MONEY | Total sales revenue generated by the product, aggregated from order details. |
